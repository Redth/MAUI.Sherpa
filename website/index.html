Find your footing

Environment Doctor ↗
Check dependencies, SDKs, and workloads. Review diagnostics and available repairs before changing your setup.
.NET SDKs & workloads ↗
Track SDK and runtime channels with dotnetup. Manage workload sets and inspect a project's .NET context.
Android SDK & Xcode ↗
Install Android packages and system images. On macOS, manage Xcode versions and simulator runtimes.

See what's happening

Devices & emulators ↗
Manage Android devices and emulators together, including USB and Wi-Fi connections, pairing, and device inspection.
DevFlow & device inspectors ↗
Explore MAUI visual trees, live properties, network requests, logs, and Blazor WebViews. Use device tools for files, shells, and captures.
Profiling & push testing ↗
Capture startup and interaction profiles, open traces and GC dumps, and test notification payloads with APNs or Firebase.

Get ready to ship

Signing, sorted ↗
Manage Android keystores and PEPK exports, plus Apple certificates, bundle IDs, registered devices, and provisioning profiles.
Local Vault & cloud secrets ↗
Keep encrypted secrets locally or use cloud providers. Choose where signing assets, secrets, and publish profiles are synchronized.
Reusable publish profiles ↗
Bundle signing configurations and secrets, review destinations, and publish to GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, or Azure DevOps.

A little help along the way. Chat with GitHub Copilot inside Sherpa for help with your development environment. Copilot sign-in and access are required.

Three platforms.
One familiar base camp.

Built with .NET MAUI, with an AppKit head on macOS, WinUI on Windows, and GTK on Linux. Pick the machine you work on, not a different set of habits.

Apple account tools work across platforms. Xcode, local Apple devices and simulators, and Apple root-certificate management require macOS. Profiling captures support Android targets and, on macOS, iOS simulators; imported traces and GC dumps can be opened across platforms.
